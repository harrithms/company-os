# Glossary

Words that mean something specific here. The point is that two people reading the same report
reach the same conclusion.

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Metric** | The one number a person is permanently accountable for |
| **Goal** | The agreed target for a metric, with a date and an owner |
| **Ratified** | A goal the owner has explicitly agreed to |
| **Proposed** | A goal written down but not yet confirmed by its owner |
| **Needs setup** | A person with no documented goal at all |
| **Key result** | A quarterly bet. Graded at quarter end. Never colours a card |
| **Stale** | An observation old enough that the colour may no longer be true |
| **Commitment** | A specific thing someone said they would do, by a date, written down |
