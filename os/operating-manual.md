# Operating manual

The spine. How the parts of this repo fit together, and in which order they constrain each other.

## The chain

```
direction        why we exist, and the one number that says it is working
    ↓
planning         this year, then this quarter's bets
    ↓
accountability   one number and one target per person
    ↓
execution        how work moves, and what stops it shipping
    ↓
reporting        when we look, and what we look at
    ↓
decisions        what we concluded, so we do not conclude it again
```

Each layer is constrained by the one above. A key result that serves no objective, or a person's
metric that serves no key result, is a sign that something upstream is missing.

## Metrics and OKRs are not the same thing

The most common confusion, so it is settled here:

- A **metric** is the job. It is true of the role next year too. It colours the person's card.
- A **key result** is this quarter's bet. It is worked first and graded at the end. It never
  colours a card.

Someone can hit every key result and still be below their metric. That is information, not a
contradiction.

## The rhythm

See `cadence.json` for the machine-readable version. The short form: weekly review, weekly
one-to-ones, monthly review at month-end close, fortnightly all-hands, quarterly planning and
retrospective, quarterly board.
