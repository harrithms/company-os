# One-to-ones

The recurring conversation between a person and their manager. Different questions weekly and monthly.

## What this file has to answer

- What is asked every week?
- What is asked monthly instead?
- Where do the notes and commitments live?
- What is never discussed here?

## Yours

_Delete everything below and write your own._

**Weekly (25 min).** What is in your way? What did you decide this week? What do you need from me?

**Monthly (45 min).** How is the number tracking, and do you still believe the target? What are
you learning? Where do you want to be in a year?

**Notes** live with the person’s card. **Commitments** are written down and reviewed next time.

**Not here.** Status reporting — that is the weekly review.
