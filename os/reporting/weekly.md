# Weekly review

The meeting that catches things while they are still small. What changed, and what needs a decision.

## What this file has to answer

- Which numbers moved, and which did not?
- What is red or two-weeks yellow, and who owns the recovery?
- What decisions are being asked for this week?
- What commitments were made last week, and were they kept?

## Yours

_Delete everything below and write your own._

**Agenda (30 min).**
1. Cards that changed colour — owner speaks, two minutes each
2. Anything red, or yellow twice — what is the commitment?
3. Decisions requested
4. Commitments from last week: kept, or not

**Rule.** No slides. The numbers are already written down; the meeting is for judgment.
