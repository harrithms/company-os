# Performance

How performance is discussed rather than avoided.

## What this file has to answer

- What is the rhythm of feedback?
- What happens when someone is consistently off their number?
- What is never a surprise?

## Yours

_Delete everything below and write your own._

**Rhythm.** Weekly one-to-one for obstacles; monthly for the number and the trend.

**Consistently off.** Two consecutive months below target starts a written plan with a date,
agreed in the one-to-one. The plan names the support, not just the target.

**Never a surprise.** Nothing appears in a review that has not already been said out loud.
