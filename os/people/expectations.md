## Yours

**Does the work.** Owns a number. Raises problems early. Finishes things.

**Owns an area.** All the above, plus decides without asking, and is the person others check
with on that area.

**Owns an outcome.** All the above, plus sets the target rather than receiving it, and is
accountable when it is missed.

**Where you stand** is said in the monthly one-to-one, not saved for a review.
