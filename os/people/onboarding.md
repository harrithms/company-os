# Onboarding

How someone becomes useful in their first fortnight.

## What this file has to answer

- What do they read, in what order?
- Who do they meet?
- What do they own by the end of week two?

## Yours

_Delete everything below and write your own._

**Day one.** `operating-manual.md`, then `direction/`. Meet their manager and set the first
one-to-one.

**Week one.** `accountability/` — see the chart, find their own card. Sit in on the weekly review.

**End of week two.** They own a number, even a small one, and it is written in `goals.md`.

---

If a new hire cannot answer "what am I accountable for" at the end of week two, onboarding did
not happen — regardless of how many meetings they attended.
