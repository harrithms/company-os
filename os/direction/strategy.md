# Strategy

The bet. Where you are choosing to compete, and what that rules out.

## What this file has to answer

- What is the bet, in one paragraph?
- Why is it available to you and not to others?
- What does it rule out — segments, features, channels you are choosing not to chase?
- What would make you abandon it?
