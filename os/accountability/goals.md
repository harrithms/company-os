# Goals

The target behind each number, who agreed it, and when. A number without an agreed target is not accountability, it is reporting.

## What this file has to answer

- What is the target for each metric?
- Who agreed it, and on what date?
- Is it ratified, or proposed and awaiting the owner?
- What is the expected shape of progress — linear, or milestones?
