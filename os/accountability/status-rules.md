# Status rules

What red, yellow and green mean. Decided once, in advance, so the colour is never argued about in the meeting.

## What this file has to answer

- How is expected progress computed at a point in the quarter?
- What thresholds separate green, yellow and red?
- What happens when a number has no target, or no data?
- How stale can an observation be before it stops counting?

## Yours

_Delete everything below and write your own._

**Expected progress.** Linear across the quarter unless the goal names milestones.

**Thresholds.** Green ≥ 95% of expected · Yellow 80–95% · Red < 80%.

**Other states.** `no-target` and `needs-setup` are shown as themselves, never as green.

**Staleness.** An observation older than 14 days is flagged; the card keeps its last colour
and says it is stale.

**Two consecutive yellows** force a conversation and a written commitment.

This is the single most valuable file here. Deciding the rule before you need it is what stops
